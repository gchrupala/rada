A Very Dangerous Christmas To You

an interactive story by Rada Alishahi

You’re at a friends party, mainly just hanging near the snacks and eating a lot. Then, a random girl walks up to you, you haven't ever seen her before. She has brown hair, little earrings, red lipstick and was wearing a medium length long sleeved black sparkly dress. She has a worried expression.

‘Hey, are you *your name*?’ She asks, looking at you.

‘Uh, yeah. That’s me!’ You say, and you laugh nervously.

‘You- we have to get out of here quickly.’ She says, and she keeps looking back, like she was scared something -somebody- was watching you.

‘What? Why?’

‘No time to explain. Please!’

What do you do?

If you decide to trust her, go to 3. If you decide to stay, go to 2.

2

You don´t trust it.

‘No… I can't come with you.’ You shake your head. ‘Sorry.’

She glares at you. ‘Well yes,’ she spits out. ‘Well do that! Kill yourself!’ She says angrily, walking away.

You feel confused. What just happened? You run back to the girl.

‘Wait! Could you at least explain what is happening?’

She looks behind you, and her eyes widen. ‘Oh my goodness.’

You turn around. You see the following: a blonde lady and brunette man talking with drinks in their hand. An old lady sulking in the corner, a boy trying to make a conversation with a pretty, dark-skinned girl. And… a middle-aged man, standing there, clutching his bag. His expression was completely blank, so you couldn’t read off his face at all.

‘Uh…’

‘Please!’

What do you do?

If you follow her, go to 4, if not, 5.

3

‘Fine. But where?’ You ask her.

‘You'll see.’ She says. She looks behind you and her eyes widen. You look around and follow her gaze, to a middle aged man with ugly, brown hair, holding a big bag.

‘Who is he?’ You ask her, but she doesn’t respond, just shakes her head.

‘Come. Quickly!’ She says as she pulls you away. You vaguely see the man following you, slowly but steady. You both run towards the exit, and you’re about to open the door when Brianna, your friend, walks over.

‘Hey, why are you going away? The party just started!’ She says, and shoots a quick glance at the girl. ‘Oh, hey Rayna. Fancy seeing you here.’ She says, putting pressure on the words so it comes out as sarcastic.

Supposed Rayna tells you, ‘Listen, we have to go. Or else you’ll-’ She cuts herself off, looking at Brianna. ‘Or something really unpleasant will happen.’

You look at Brianna apologetically, but what are you supposed to do?

If you (quite brutally) ignore Brianna, and leave trusting the Rayna girl, go 6. Are you staying, go to 5.

4

‘Oh alright, fine, but where are we going?’

‘No time, they’re here already!’ She says. The man approaches you slowly. The girl pulls you to the door. You both run to the exit, but then the path is blocked by Rebecca, a girl you vaguely remember from school.

‘Hey, you can’t leave yet! Jesus, the party just started.’ She says, rolling her eyes. She notices the girl and her face scrunches up with disgust. ‘Oh yes, Rayna is here.’ She said the word “Rayna” like it was something stuck under her shoe.

The girl -Rayna?- looks at you with panic.

‘Listen, we have to go. Or else… some unpleasant things will happen.’ She ended, like she was purposely avoiding the word “die” or “death”.

What do you do?

If you (quite brutally) shove past Rebecca and trust Rayna, go to 6. If you decide to stay, go to 5.

5

‘No, sorry I can’t go. You might be mad, I’m staying.’

She looks at you with angry and hurt eyes.

‘Alright. I do not care.’ And with that, she marches away.

You go back to the snack buffet, confused. When you grab a brownie, the middle-aged man reaches you, and takes out a stick out of his bag. It suddenly turns into a sword, and he pokes you in the chest. It hurts. You die.

THE TRAGIC END

Start again

6

‘Okay, let’s go.’ You say, and you push through the door, not caring if there’s anybody standing there. You both run outside. Fresh air immediately hits your face, which is nice since you were trapped in that party for longer than an hour (no idea how Rebecca got the ‘just started’ quote).

‘Follow me! Rayna says, and you do, into the forest. You start to wonder if this was indeed such a good idea.

‘So can you explain what is happening?’

She sighs. ‘Alright, so here’s the thing:’ And then she explains.

‘At school, I was walking towards my locker. But on my way, I saw a shadow going towards the attic. I thought it just was some kind of teacher, but I decided to follow anyway. I figured that if I got caught, I’d just lie and say I got lost. So when I went up the stairs, I saw them entering a room that the school announced “Out of bounds” and nobody was allowed to go inside. It even had a little sign that said: “Hey, sorry, this room is out of bounds! Students are not allowed in under any circumstances.” With a picture of a chicken.’

Rayna rolled her eyes.

‘But anyway, I knew it was risky, but decided to enter anyway. What I saw inside…’ She shuddered. ‘It was a mass-murder plan.’

‘A what?’

‘So a bunch of people, maybe fifty or sixty, I don’t know, they were planning to attack a person, then claim to be that person’s family, fabricate the will and leave everything to them. They would use that money to buy “do I know how many” guns or something.’

‘And?’

‘They just chose a random rich person, which happens to be you. I knew that if they managed to kill you, the whole city would be in danger. Oh, and it’d be pleasant if you didn’t die either.’

‘Gee, thanks.’

‘And the worst thing,’ she continued, ignoring you. She leaned in closer. ‘That they’re monsters.’

‘You know, I kind of figured that out already…’

‘No, real monsters. Magical ones.’

You laugh. ‘Yes, I-’ You get cut off by a very loud growling coming from behind.

What do you do?

If you run, go to 7. If you distract the monster and hide, go to 8.

7

‘Run!’ You say, so you both run deeper into the forest. But then your leg hooks onto a branch. You scream for Rayna, but she falls. The monster reaches her and eats her.

‘Rayna!’

But then the monster eats you too. You die. Pretty dumb, huh?

THE TRAGIC END

Start again

8

You grab a stone.

‘What are you doing?’ Rayna whispers. But you just shake your head. You throw it with full strength, it lands somewhere around 40 metres away. The growling stops and you hear shuffling to that direction.

‘Hide!’ You hiss. You both hide in a big bush, somewhere deep in the forest. Half a minute later you hear horrible growling, and the monster shuffling far away. Yes! You both quietly get out of the bush.

‘So what are we doing now?’ You ask Rayna.

‘I know a village nearby. It’s safe. Let’s go!’

You hear the monster roaring again, that’s when you ran like you never ran before.

20 minutes later you arrive. It’s a small but beautiful place. The trees are beautifully blossoming, even though it was Christmas, in the middle of the winter. But somehow that gave a beautiful, magical vibe, when the pretty pink flowers mixed with the pearl white snow. The houses are made out of wood, and give a nice, cosy vibe. You immediately fall in love with this place.

You smile softly. ‘What is it called?’

‘Honnerdome.’ Rayna says. ‘Are you staying here? It is monster-free.’

You laugh. You have no idea how the rest of life will go on. But for now, you feel happy.

THE HAPPY END

Bravo! You survived! Well done. Now you can just take a different decision and it’s a whole new story!
